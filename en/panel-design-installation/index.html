Electrical Panel Design and Installation

We design and install safe, IEC-compliant industrial and building-type panels tailored to your facility. Panel components come from our own stock of brands such as Siemens, Schneider Electric, Legrand and Entes.

Ordering process

  1. RequirementsWe listen to where the panel will be used and what you need.
  2. QuotationWe prepare a technical and price quotation for the panel.
  3. ManufactureProduction starts once the quotation is approved.
  4. Delivery and installationWe deliver the finished panel and install it on site if required.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a panel order take?

Lead time depends on the type and size of the panel; we confirm it with the quotation.

Which brands do you use in panels?

Depending on the requirement, we use switchgear and panel components from Siemens, Schneider Electric, Legrand, Entes and similar brands.

Do you ship panels outside Istanbul?

Yes, we deliver work and materials to projects outside Istanbul as well.
