Data we process

The name, company, phone number, email address, city/district and request details you enter in the quote form.

Purpose and legal basis

We use your data only to assess your quote request, send you prices and quotations, and contact you about them. The legal basis is Article 5(2)(c) of the KVKK: processing directly related to entering into a contract.

Transfers

The form uses the Web3Forms service to deliver messages, which arrive in our company's Gmail (Google) mailbox. Because these providers' servers are located outside Türkiye, your data is transferred abroad on the basis of the explicit consent you give when submitting the form. We do not share your data with any other third party or use it for marketing.

Retention

Quote requests are deleted once the request is concluded and any legal retention obligations have expired.

Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK you may ask whether your data is processed, request information, ask for correction or deletion, and object to processing. Send requests to erencanelektrik@gmail.com.
