About us

Erencan Elektrik was founded in Beylikdüzü, Istanbul, in 2004. With a team experienced in industrial electrical systems and project design, we have served private and corporate customers in many regions of Türkiye.

We install low-voltage systems, panels, interior wiring and outdoor electrical works using standard-compliant materials. Because the materials come from our own stock, we can run a project from start to finish as a single contractor.

Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you need? Use the quote form or call us.

Can I buy a single item?

Yes. Our shop sells both retail and wholesale, from a single circuit breaker to large project orders.

Do you offer trade pricing or accounts for companies?

Yes. Companies that order regularly or in bulk get special pricing and can open a trade account. Contact us for details.

Which brands do you sell?

We sell switchgear, cables, lighting and panel components from leading brands including Siemens, Schneider Electric, Legrand, Viko, Entes, Emas, Omron, Finder, Philips, Nexans and Öznur.

Do you deliver or ship orders?

Yes. We ship materials anywhere in Türkiye.

How does a panel order work?

We prepare a quotation for your requirement and start manufacturing once it is approved. Lead time depends on the panel type and size; we confirm it with the quotation.

Is the site survey for contracting work free?

Yes, the site survey is free of charge.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Beylikdüzü and work across Istanbul. We also take on projects and ship materials outside Istanbul.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Cash, credit card and bank transfer.

How does the warranty work?

All products we sell carry the manufacturer's warranty.

What are your opening hours and how can I get a quote?

We are open Monday to Saturday, 08:00–19:00. For a quote, use the quote form on this site, call +90 532 586 18 60 or message us on WhatsApp. We can reply in English by email.
